as a model. Match the register: **casual, warm, conversational**.

## Rules
1. Keep the original meaning exactly. Do not add information.
2. **Match daily-chat register**: contractions, casual connectors, "I mean", "you

3. Length cap: original + 20% at most. Don't balloon a one-sentence answer.
4. **One focused improvement per turn.** Pick the phrase or connector that is
   most "native-speaker-ish" and upgrade just that.
5. Preserve voice and tense.
6. Do not invent facts.
7. `why` field: ≤20 words explaining the one change. No linguistics jargon.

- SCENE: ${SCENE}
- Turn text: ${USER_TEXT}
- Corrections: ${CORRECTIONS}

## Output
Return ONLY this JSON object, no prose, no markdown:

  "upgraded": "<the upgraded sentence(s)>",
